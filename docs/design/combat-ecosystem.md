# Combat Ecosystem

## Core Philosophy

Combat is not about creating one unstoppable build.

Combat is about adapting to changing situations.

The strongest player should still occasionally need to change tactics.

---

## Damage Types

Damage should be modular and easily expandable.

Possible examples:

- Physical
- Fire
- Ice
- Electricity
- Poison
- Corrosion
- Radiation
- Laser
- Plasma
- Explosive

Additional damage types should be easy to introduce through data rather than code changes.

---

## Enemy Resistances

Every enemy may define:

- resistances
- weaknesses
- immunities
- vulnerabilities

Examples:

Robot

- immune to poison
- vulnerable to corrosion

Crystal Creature

- reflects lasers
- weak against kinetic damage

Organic Beast

- resistant to corrosion
- vulnerable to radiation

The purpose is to encourage varied builds rather than one universal solution.

---

## Build Counterplay

Every powerful build should eventually encounter enemies that require adaptation.

Examples:

- laser-resistant enemies
- explosive-resistant enemies
- highly mobile enemies
- shielded enemies
- enemies using cover
- enemies that dodge slow projectiles

Players should always have reasons to diversify their arsenal.

---

## Status Effects

Status effects should interact with enemy types.

Examples:

Fire

- burn
- panic

Corrosion

- armor reduction
- structural damage

Radiation

- mutation
- health degeneration
- biological disruption

Electricity

- stun
- chain damage

Future interactions between effects are encouraged.

---

## Melee System

The player always has access to a basic melee attack.

Initial implementation:

- simple kick
- knockback
- long cooldown
- low damage

Future upgrades may transform the melee attack into entirely different abilities.

Examples:

- cybernetic punch
- holy strike
- energy blade
- beast claws

---

## Throwables

Throwables are not only combat tools.

They should also serve exploration.

Possible uses:

- opening hidden passages
- destroying statues
- activating ancient mechanisms
- solving environmental puzzles

Examples:

- grenades
- EMP devices
- acid flasks
- holy relics
- dimensional charges

---

## Boss Philosophy

Bosses should challenge the player's understanding of mechanics rather than simply their DPS.

Bosses should reward preparation, observation, and adaptation.

Every boss should ideally test different aspects of the player's build.
